Alignment, not certification. How we apply each one.

Seven steps, in three phases.

Decide, build, compound. You can see where the work sits at every step.

  1. AuditWeeks 1 to 2. We read the last 12 months and price every leak.
  2. DecideWeek 3. We pick the few fixes worth making and say why the rest can wait.
  3. BlueprintWeeks 4 to 5. A written plan with owners, controls and how each one is measured.
  4. BuildFrom week 6. The smallest working fix goes live first, and you can roll it back.
  5. AutomateIn parallel. Automations chase what is owed. A person signs off anything that touches money.
  6. TrainFinal third. Your team learns by doing the work alongside us.
  7. Hand-offFinal two weeks. Full source, documents and a monthly statement listing each item recovered.
